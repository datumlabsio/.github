# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be someone people want to work with. Argue about the work, not the person.

## What that means here

- **Disagree in the open, on the work.** Say what you think is wrong and why. "This is wrong because X" is welcome. "You always do this" is not.
- **Assume competence.** The person who wrote the thing you are questioning usually had a reason. Ask what it was before deciding there wasn't one.
- **Own your calls.** If you shipped it, it is yours — including when an agent wrote the first draft. Blaming the tool is not available.
- **Explain instead of gatekeeping.** Somebody not knowing a thing yet is not a character flaw. If you know it, hand it over.
- **No harassment, no discrimination, no personal attacks.** Not in issues, PRs, commit messages, review comments, or anywhere else attached to this org.

## Reporting

Tell either founder — [@zainulabidin302](https://github.com/zainulabidin302) or [@ifaizankhan](https://github.com/ifaizankhan). If it involves one of them, tell the other.

Reports are handled privately. You will not be penalised for making one in good faith.

## Scope

Every repo in the `datumlabsio` organization, and anywhere else people are acting on behalf of Datum Labs.
